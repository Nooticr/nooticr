# Nooticr

**Social intelligence for AI assistants — read, monitor, find talent, and create across ten social networks.**

[nooticr.com](https://nooticr.com) gives an AI assistant real access to social
media: it can **read** real posts across ten networks (TikTok, Instagram,
YouTube, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Bilibili), **follow**
a brand — every mention, including what's said out loud on video, not just
typed — **find** the right creators and collaborators from real data rather
than a guess, and **create** from what it learns: hooks, variants to film, a
scored draft, a repurposed thread. It's a real MCP server any AI assistant
can connect to directly.

## Who it's for

Anyone whose AI assistant needs to actually see social media rather than
guess at it — creators and marketers who want their assistant to read what's
working, watch for every mention of their brand, find who to work with next,
and help write the next thing.

## What you can do

- **Read** — posts, creators, trends, comments, transcripts and video frames
  across ten networks
- **Follow a brand** — every mention, across networks and spoken word, inside
  a date window you choose, plus a standing watch that reports what's new
- **Find talent** — search creators by niche, surface lookalikes of one that
  already fits, and shortlist collaborators from real follower and bio data
- **Create** — hooks, variants, scored drafts, repurposed copy, grounded in
  what actually worked, not invented
