# Orchyn

**Short-form video scheduling & native social automation for product marketers.**

[orchyn.com](https://orchyn.com) — plan, compose, and ship product videos across social without the agency overhead.

## What Orchyn does

Orchyn helps product marketing teams turn briefs into scheduled short-form content: compose clips, automate native posting, and keep distribution in one workflow.

- **Compose** — assemble short-form video from product assets and prompts
- **Schedule** — plan releases across channels from one calendar
- **Automate** — publish natively to social without copy-paste ops
